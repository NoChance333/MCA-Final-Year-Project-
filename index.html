GUIDE Q&A DEFENSE CHEAT SHEET

Q: Why did nobody use DQN + Deep Autoencoders together?

Answer: Literature was strictly split. Autoencoder researchers (Fei, Babatunde, Zhang) used AE only for clustering and used static single-hop or Dijkstra to the sink. RL researchers (Kanakis, Padma Priya) used DQN on flat networks or naive Euclidean K-Means. We are the first to unify both into a dual-stage architecture solving spatial distortion (DCAE) and multi-hop routing (DQN) simultaneously.

Q: Why is Energy Efficiency the primary objective?

Answer: Sensors operate on finite batteries in remote fields. When battery exhausts, the node permanently dies, collapsing topology and dropping throughput to zero. Energy efficiency directly determines network survival.

Q: Why does LEACH fail over large terrain?

Answer: LEACH forces Cluster Heads to transmit directly to the base station (500m). RF attenuation follows d⁴ multipath path loss. 1 packet over 500m consumes 0.53 Joules, exhausting battery in 15 rounds. Our DQN constrains hops to <= 140m (d² free space zone, 0.003 J/pkt), saving 170x energy.
